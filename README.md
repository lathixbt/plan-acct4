A plan an AI agent can carry out itself to turn $100 into the most money in 30 days: scrape dental clinics from Google Maps, verify every email and phone, enrich each lead with owner and website, and sell clean 500-lead lists at $99 to marketing agencies via Gumroad and cold email.

Run `main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) — it writes the plan to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
